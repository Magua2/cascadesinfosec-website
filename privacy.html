Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Cascades Infosec Systems LLC ("we", "us") collects through cascadesinfosec.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send our contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, company name (if given), the service you're interested in, and your message.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss our services with you.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for their own marketing.

Services we rely on

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log technical data such as your IP address for security and reliability.
  • Namecheap Private Email hosts our email.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship with you, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@cascadesinfosec.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
