Approach

How an engagement works

  1. Discover

    We learn how your business runs and map the systems and data that matter most.

  2. Assess

    We test controls and configurations against real-world threats and your compliance needs.

  3. Report

    You get a plain-language report with prioritized findings and a remediation plan.

    We help fix what we found, then retest to confirm the gaps are closed.
